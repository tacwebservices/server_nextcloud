# Welcome to MyStickCloud ☁️

Your USB stick, in the cloud. Store, share and back up your files from any phone or laptop.

- **Upload:** drag files into this window, or use the **+ New** button.
- **Share:** click the share icon next to a file to send a link or share it with someone.
- **Sync:** install the app on your phone or computer to keep your files with you everywhere.

You can edit or delete this note at any time.
